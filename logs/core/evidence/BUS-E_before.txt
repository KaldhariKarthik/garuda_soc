Mechanism: a JAL / predicted-taken branch in ID redirects fetch to its misaligned target;
the instruction then waits in EX behind a load in MEM (its trap or mispredict redirect is
held while the D-port transfer is outstanding, T-9), and the I-port, already redirected,
issues a word fetch at the misaligned address. Architecturally harmless (the slots are
bubbles; Spike lockstep MATCH on the traps), but an AHB-Lite protocol violation: a slave may
answer ERROR or misbehave, and the target can be any address (a peripheral).
