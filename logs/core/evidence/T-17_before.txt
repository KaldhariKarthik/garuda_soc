Mechanism: after the trap flushes the prefetch buffer, its empty slot 0 still holds the
fault bit; IF/ID and ID/EX carry it on a bubble (valid=0, fault=1), and trap_ctrl raises
cause 1 from that bubble (e1 = idex_fault_i, not qualified by idex_valid_i). The redirect
flushes again, slot 0 still holds the bit: endless loop. Same class as ERRATUM T-4.
