# SoC verification: the G6 record (flow stages F7 and F8 for the chip)

1-TOPS gate G6, rows 6.1 to 6.19 (6.9, 6.10 and 6.11 closed on 2026-11-03 and 2026-11-04).
Worked on 2026-11-20 and 2026-11-21, trees `7bdc738` to `cc4325f`. One RTL change in that
time: **SPIM-6** (`cc4325f`, a vendor patch to the SPI master controller, section 3), found by
the 6.4 work; every row whose run was on the RTL from before it says so.

Simulators: Xcelium 22.09 (`xrun`, the plain bench `tb/soc/tb_chip.sv`, `make test_chip`) and
irun 15.20 with UVM CDNS-1.2 (the UVM chip bench `tb/chip_uvm`, `make uvm_chip`, and the
coverage runs of `flow/regress/garuda.vsif`); coverage read in IMC 15.20. Formal: JasperGold
Apps 2022.09. The run records are in `flow/signoff/runs/` and are named below by their date
and name only (`2026-11-21_chip_regs` = `flow/signoff/runs/2026-11-21_chip_regs.txt`).

Every chip program returns 0 or the number of the step that failed, and every new one was
shown to fail: each record lists the planted defects (each in its own copy of `rtl/`) and the
step that caught them.
