CLOCK DOMAIN, ONLY WHEN A BLOCK IS LINTED ON ITS OWN (dma_top, timers_top, ahb2apb_bridge)
  CLKDMN  dma_top.u_apb.prdata_q, timers_top.u_apb.prdata_q, ahb2apb_bridge.hrdata_o
          One each. Linted alone, hclk_i and pclk_i are two unrelated input
          ports, so HAL reports the hclk/pclk boundary as a crossing. In the chip
          pclk is hclk divided by two in clk_div, edge-aligned, so the two are
          one synchronous domain and no synchroniser is wanted (AHB2APB R-4,
          DMA R-3). The chip-level run does not report them. Evidence that the
          clocks are aligned: tb_crg t_pclk_div2. Not yet held by an assertion:
          a_pclk_on_hclk and a_capture_stable_at_phase are specified but not
          written (BUGS_HISTORY.md AUD-11).

WARNINGS ACCEPTED BY NAME (added 2026-10-14)
  BLKSQB  rtl/clk_div/clk_div.v  pclk_q, two lines
  RDBFAS  rtl/clk_div/clk_div.v  pclk_q
          The pclk toggle flop is assigned with a blocking assignment on purpose
          (BUGS SIM-1, header of the block in clk_div.v). pclk is a clock made
          from hclk; with a non-blocking assignment its edge reached the
          simulator one step after every hclk flop had changed, and a pclk flop
          capturing from an hclk flop saw the new value, where silicon with
          balanced clock trees sees the old one. No other flop in the design is
          written this way, and no logic reads pclk_q as data: pclk_phase has a
          flop of its own. Synthesis builds the same toggle flop.
