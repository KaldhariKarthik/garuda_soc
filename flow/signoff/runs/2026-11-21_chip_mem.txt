G6 6.12 (bare-metal C suite: memory march and walking ones): t_chip_mem, 2026-11-21
RTL as at e756c4c (no RTL change); Xcelium 22.09, plain tb_chip

From the core through the AHB fabric, over the memory the program does not use (sw/chip/link_chip.ld):
DSRAM 0x2000_1000-0x2000_CFFF (all four banks), ISRAM 0x0000_4000-0x0000_FFFF.
  1 data bus: walking ones and zeros in one word of every bank
  2 address lines 2..15: a unique word at base + 2^k, each line written alone, none may alias
  3 byte and halfword lanes: each lane alone, the word read back
  4 March C- over the whole region (up w0; up r0 w1; up r1 w0; down r0 w1; down r1 w0; up r0)
