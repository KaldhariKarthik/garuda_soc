G6 6.4 (peripheral receive and transmit paths at chip level): t_chip_periph steps 5 and 7, 2026-11-21
RTL: 339a0d8 + SPIM-6 (vendor patch 0001, spim_ctrl eot on a transfer with no data phase); Xcelium 22.09
plain tb_chip, and irun 15.2 UVM chip bench

Until today t_chip_periph only wrote over I2C and only read over the SPI master (the boot path).
  step 5  I2C receive: pointer write, REPEATED START, address-read, one byte with NACK + STOP;
          RXDATA must be the byte step 4 wrote to the board's slave (0xC3 at pointer 0x55)
  step 7  SPI master transmit: WREN, PAGE PROGRAM of one word to the board's flash at 0x010F00,
          READ it back; every transfer completes on IRQSTAT[0] as SPIM [N-6.1a] says
The flash model gained WREN / PAGE PROGRAM / RDSR, and tb_chip now fails on any flash protocol
violation (TB-A22; until today it counted them and nothing read the count).

== found: SPIM-6 (P2, RTL, fixed). A transfer with no data phase (WREN, a bare command, command +
   address) never raised end-of-transfer: the upstream controller went back to IDLE silently and
   IRQSTAT[0] never set. Step 7 timed out at the WREN with 8 SCLK on the wire. Fixed in the vendor
   patch (D-22): eot on the CMD->IDLE and ADDR->IDLE arcs. Docs/bugs/track_b.md, SPIM spec [N-6.1a].
