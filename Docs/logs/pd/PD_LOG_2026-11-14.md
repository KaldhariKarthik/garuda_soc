## 2. Hclk spread: cause found
On pnr_r8 the arrival times of hclk at its 4,406 sinks (SS, extracted) fall in two groups: 1,896 at
about 750 ps (bus, DMA, bridge, memory interfaces: hclk without gating) and 2,455 at 1,000 to
1,040 ps (the core, behind the ICG). The gated subtree is not balanced against the ungated one.
`-balance_levels` did not change it: OpenROAD prints `[WARNING CTS-0132] -balance_levels is obsolete`
and pnr_r9 has the same two groups (734 to 1,037 ps). Still open (PD-15).

after CTS at FF, and routing took 3.2 ps of it.
