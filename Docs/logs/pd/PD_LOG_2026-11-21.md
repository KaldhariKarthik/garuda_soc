# GARUDA: physical design on ASAP7, 2026-11-21 (run pnr_r10)

Scope: the final ASAP7 fixes before the PDK: the hclk spread (PD-15) and the FF hold residual
(PD-12), on the RTL after the F4 changes. EXPLORATORY: nothing here applies to 28 nm.
Base: `main` at `e49e5d5`. Machine: WSL default, 7.4 GB RAM, 2 GB swap, 16 processors.

## 1. The flow after F4 (PD-16)
The F4 RTL changes (SystemVerilog packages, renames) had stopped ORFS synthesis
(`garuda_pkg.sv:5: syntax error`). Fixed with Yosys's slang front end and the new divider flop names
in the SDC generator (`9a7d451`, `f135a5c`, `a9980fd`). Checked on synth_r3: every SDC pattern
resolves, the 8 clocks have their previous waveforms, the 12 unconstrained endpoints are the
synchroniser inputs.

## 2. CTS with the latency balancer (pnr_r10a, PD-15)
Without `-no_insertion_delay`, OpenROAD's latency balancer ran (CTS-0033 on pclk 45, hclk 17, clk_t2 10,
aon_clk 1 delay buffers; 73 in all) and did not repeat PD-7 (3,966 hold buffers at CTS, pnr_r9 4,184).
The hclk spread stayed 300.0 ps (793.7 to 1,093.7 ps; `run_hclk_spread.sh`). The driver rejected it
and built CTS with `-no_insertion_delay` (pnr_r10b: spread 295.0 ps). Conclusion: no CTS option of this
OpenROAD balances the gated core against the ungated sinks.

## 3. Hold repair with a 20 ps target (PD-12)
From pnr_r10b's CTS: scenes div2_WC, div2_BC, div16_BC (with the synchronous divider the DIV4..16 FF
scenes behave alike), `MMHOLD_MARGIN=20`: 5,633 endpoints below the target, 8,038 hold buffers
(pnr_r9: 4,372 with 10 ps), 1 h 33 min.

STA after CTS (12 scenes, 30 ps hold uncertainty, ±5 % OCV): all met.

## 4. Two defects of `run_pnr.sh --from` (first use of that path)
1. It wrote `run_pnr.log` into the new logs directory and then refused to copy into it ("exists").
2. It removed `5_*`/`6_*` from the hardened memory blocks too, so the corner models had no
   `6_final.odb`. Both fixed (`1c6ae59`, `8c5d147`).

## 5. Detailed route did not fit in 7.4 GB
Detailed route at 4 threads: the memory guard stopped it at 21:48 (234 MiB available, 1,256 MiB swap)
and restarted it at 2 threads; at 2 threads available memory fell to 331 MiB by 22:08 and the job was
stopped by the session's low-memory protection. The 8,038 extra hold buffers make the design larger
than pnr_r9's, which routed at 8 threads in 12 GB. Saved: `4_cts.odb` (after the hold repair) and
`5_1_grt.odb` (global route). Next: WSL with 12 GB (`.wslconfig`, D-30) and the route again from
`5_1_grt`.
